# Vision

## One sentence

Make Kenshi feel **alive and goal-rich for solo players** by owning new systems’ *logic* in a slim World State Manager and projecting only what the player can see into the existing engine.

## Why this exists

Vanilla Kenshi is brilliant and empty at the same time:

- Towns and taverns feel static.
- Slavery is a major theme with shallow player-as-slave gameplay.
- Stealth/thievery go from useless to broken with little interesting middle.
- Water is underused; travel and camping are thin.
- “What do I do today?” has few short loops that pay modest cats and story.

We are **not** building a second Kenshi or an official sequel. We are **extending** underdeveloped systems with community reverse-engineering tools.

## Pillars

1. **Alive, not busywork** — Schedules, restocks, rumors, and faces that react to state.
2. **Solo agency** — Camps, job boards, slavery escapes, stealth tools that reward planning.
3. **Extend, don’t replace** — Prefer AI packages, dialogue, inventory, anim state over new physics.
4. **Player-proximity** — Deep sim near the player; cheap tables far away.
5. **Shippable slices** — Standalone Workshop mods first; bundle later.
6. **Debug by design** — Single write path (Mutate), event history, cvars default OFF, one hotkey menu.

## Product name

- Repo / product: **Kenshi Overhaul Suite**
- Avoid: “Kenshi 2.0”, bare “Kenshi” as repo name
- Packages: Foundation, Alive Core, Survival (fishing/camps), Slavery Deep, Shadow, Voyage

## Success looks like

- A player can spend a day fishing, camping, taking a tavern job, fencing goods, and escaping a slave camp — and the world *remembers* enough that towns feel less hollow.
- Modders can enable one package without the whole suite.
- Nothing requires always-on simulation of the entire map.

## Non-goals

- Multiplayer
- Full conquest / faction takeover simulation
- True vehicle physics / second navmesh
- Full Unreal WSM port
- Monetized Steam Workshop sales (not available); optional tips later
